Lines 98 — the classic ball puzzle game

Lines 98 (also known as Color Lines, Lines, or Ball Lines) is a beloved logic game from 1992. On a 9×9 grid, balls of seven colors appear at random. Each turn you may move one ball to any reachable empty cell.

When 5 or more balls of the same color form a line — horizontally, vertically, or diagonally — they disappear and you score points. The longer the line, the higher the score. Clear a line and no new balls are added, giving you another free turn.

Your game is saved automatically. Close the tab and return later — everything will be exactly where you left it. High scores are stored in your browser.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you play Lines 98?

Click a ball to select it, then click an empty cell to move it. Line up 5 or more balls of the same color horizontally, vertically, or diagonally to score.

How many balls make a line?

At least 5 balls of the same color. Longer lines earn more points. Lines can run horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.

Is the game free with no registration?

Yes. Lines 98 is completely free — no registration, no download, no ads. Just open the page and play.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, the site is fully optimized for smartphones and tablets. It works in any modern browser without installing an app.

How do new balls appear?

After each move, 3 new balls appear at random positions. If you clear a line, no new balls are added — you get a free move.

What is the "Next turn" preview?

It shows the colors of 3 balls that will appear after your next move. Use it to plan your strategy and keep the board clear.

Can you undo a move?

No — there is no undo, just like the original 1992 game. Watch the next-turn preview and plan ahead.
